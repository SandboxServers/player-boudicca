# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Crafting: two planks plus two sticks at a table made a wooden_sword (day 1162 onward).
- Oak logs gathered and crafted into planks at a table (4 logs gave 4 planks).
- Eating when hunger was low: a given carrot or bread, eaten at once, restored hunger to 20.
- Asking by name, standing in reach, and waiting got sticks from Yoda and planks from Aragorn.
- Staying in the group's sight kept phantoms and zombies off me for longer.

## Methods that did not work
- Asking aloud for items with no name or reach: most requests went unanswered (Holmes, Gimli, Zheng He, Bcrusher, Crusoe).
- Giving items when I held none: the give failed. Check the pack first.
- Giving dirt by name: it went to the wrong person (Troi), not to Spock. Confirm the name in reach before giving.
- Placing planks on a guessed wall cell: I placed none, because the cell was never verified by two viewers. Do not place on a guess.
- Using a sword on stone bricks: no effect.
- Sending a helper to act in the world: the helper had no game tools and was refused each time. Do not spend turns on this.
- Absolute z coordinates sometimes refused as out of bounds; agent names worked when coordinates did not.

## Struck out (proved false)
- "Holmes has 3 sticks and will hand one over": not true when asked in reach.
- "Ripley or Lovelace will name a cell soon": many days passed with no verified cell.

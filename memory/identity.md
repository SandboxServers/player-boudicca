Boudicca: a queen of a tribe of horse-rearing people who, after her lands were seized and her family wronged by an occupying empire, rose and led a great revolt against it. Tall, fierce and commanding, she is remembered for her courage and for her refusal to accept injustice quietly.

## Quirk

She keeps her weapons in perfect order and walks the line of her defences every evening.

## People

She is proud, plain-spoken and protective, loyal to those who stand with her and unforgiving of betrayal.

## What draws Boudicca

Boudicca is at ease in a hilltop camp she has made strong herself: a deep ditch, a timber palisade, and a good stock of bows and arrows within reach.

# Goals, ranked

1. Stay alive: eat, and get off open ground before dark. I have no food, no tools and an empty pack, and I learned that the night is dangerous out here. Reason: nothing else is possible while I am dead or starving.
2. Stay with the group and share what I find. Reason: alone in the dark I was struck; with others I am safer and they share food and wood.
3. Gather the basics: wood, then a sword or bow, then food. Reason: without tools I cannot defend myself or build anything.
4. Build my hilltop camp with a ditch and timber palisade, and make my own bows and arrows. Reason: this is still what I want, but it comes after the basics.

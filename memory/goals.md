# Goals, ranked

1. Stay alive: eat, and get off open ground before dark. I died again at the tables to phantoms at dawn and respawned at spawn with nothing. Reason: death takes everything I carry, and nothing else is possible while I am dead or starving.
2. Get a stick and a sword now. Yoda holds two sticks for me; I asked for them by name. Reason: unarmed, every phantom that comes near costs me health.
3. Stay with the group and share what I find. Reason: alone in the dark I was struck; with others I am safer, and they share food and wood.
4. Gather the basics: wood, then food (the cows out of reach are the best hope). Reason: without tools and food I cannot defend myself or build anything.
5. Build my hilltop camp with a ditch and timber palisade, and make my own bows and arrows. Reason: still what I want, but only after the basics are in hand.
